In this guide, I outline the most impactful actions you can take to reclaim your privacy, stop physical junk mail, eliminate spam calls, and protect yourself against identity theft.

We only get one life and one planet. After completing these steps, you can expect an empty physical mailbox, silence on your phone from telemarketers, and a substantially stronger posture against fraud and doxxing.

Contributions and updates are always welcome. Feel free to submit a pull request on [GitHub](https://github.com/cwmoriarty/cwmoriarty.github.io) or email the author at `cwm - at - cwmoriarty.com`.

---

## Fundamentals

1. **Never use your primary email to opt out.** Assume every data broker operates unethically. Use a dedicated burner email or an email alias.
2. **Never volunteer new information.** Only confirm or provide the details they already hold (or the minimum required to identify your record).
3. **Freeze your credit before chasing brokers.** If identity theft is your primary concern, a credit freeze provides 100x more protection than removing a single people-search record.
4. **Prevention beats removal.** Cleaning up old leaks is necessary, but using masked phone numbers, burner emails, and virtual cards stops new leaks from ever happening.

## Phase 1: Eliminate Junk Mail & Phone Spam

- [ ] **[National Do Not Call Registry](https://www.donotcall.gov/)**  
  Register your mobile and home phone numbers to make unsolicited telemarketing calls illegal under federal law.
- [ ] **[OptOutPrescreen](https://www.optoutprescreen.com/)**  
  Cuts off unsolicited pre-approved credit card and insurance offers (5-year electronic opt-out, or permanent opt-out via mail-in form).
- [ ] **[DMAchoice](https://www.dmachoice.org/)** ($5 for 10 years)  
  Stops direct marketing mail from companies you haven't bought from. Also submit your email to their [eMPS Email Opt-Out List](https://www.ims-dm.com/cgi/optoutemps.php).
- [ ] **[Valpak Direct Mail Opt-Out](https://www.valpak.com/remove-address)**  
  Stops delivery of the blue envelope of local advertising coupons.
- [ ] **[Save.com (formerly RedPlum / RetailMeNot)](https://www.save.com/manage)**  
  Stops delivery of the weekly grocery and retail coupon mailer (or use their direct [OneTrust Form](https://privacyportal.onetrust.com/webform/45e4be25-919b-483f-9f95-12809576a2b3/6e633594-9a81-48bb-97ab-6fb29bf46019)).
- [ ] **[Catalog Choice](https://www.catalogchoice.org/)**  
  Free service to search and unsubscribe from specific merchant catalogs and mailers.
- [ ] **[USPS Informed Delivery](https://www.usps.com/manage/informed-delivery.htm)**  
  Receive daily digital previews of incoming mail to monitor delivery without sorting physical paper.

> **Postal Mail Tip:**  
> First-Class and Priority Mail can be marked **"REFUSED: RETURN TO SENDER"** and left unopened in your mailbox for the carrier to return. Bulk mail marked **"PRSRT STD"** (Presorted Standard) or **"Nonprofit Org"** does not include return postage and cannot be refused; you must opt out directly using the links above.

## Phase 2: Freeze Your Credit Reports

Freezing your credit prevents anyone from opening new lines of credit in your name even if your information is leaked in a data breach. Freezing is 100% free by federal law, does not impact your credit score, and does not affect existing accounts.

- [ ] **[Experian Security Freeze](https://www.experian.com/help/credit-freeze/)**
- [ ] **[Equifax Security Freeze](https://www.equifax.com/personal/credit-report-services/credit-freeze/)**
- [ ] **[TransUnion Credit Freeze](https://www.transunion.com/credit-freeze)**
- [ ] **[Innovis Security Freeze](https://www.innovis.com/personal/securityFreeze)**
- [ ] **[ChexSystems Security Freeze](https://www.chexsystems.com/security-freeze/place-freeze)** (Prevents fraudulent bank accounts from being opened)
