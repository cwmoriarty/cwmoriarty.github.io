# 📭 cwmoriarty's Guide to Opting Out

In this guide, I outline the most impactful actions you can take to reclaim your privacy, stop physical junk mail, eliminate spam calls, and protect yourself against identity theft.

We only get one life and one planet. After completing these steps, you can expect an empty physical mailbox, silence on your phone from telemarketers, and a substantially stronger posture against fraud and doxxing.

Contributions and updates are always welcome. Feel free to submit a pull request on [GitHub](https://github.com/cwmoriarty/cwmoriarty.github.io) or email the author at `cwm - at - cwmoriarty.com`.

## 🧭 The 4 Golden Rules of Opting Out

Before you begin, keep these four principles in mind:

1. **Never use your primary email to opt out.** Assume every data broker operates unethically. Use a dedicated burner email or an email alias (e.g., via Cloaked, SimpleLogin, or Apple Hide My Email).
2. **Never volunteer new information.** Only confirm or provide the details they already hold (or the minimum required to identify your record).
3. **Freeze your credit before chasing brokers.** If identity theft is your primary concern, a credit freeze provides 100x more protection than removing a single people-search record.
4. **Prevention beats removal.** Cleaning up old leaks is necessary, but using masked phone numbers, burner emails, and virtual cards stops new leaks from ever happening.

## Phase 1: Immediate Wins — Eliminate Junk Mail & Phone Spam
> **Estimated time:** 15–20 minutes  
> **Impact:** ~80% reduction in physical mail clutter and robocalls within 2–6 weeks.

- [ ] **[National Do Not Call Registry](https://www.donotcall.gov/)**  
  Register your mobile and home phone numbers. It takes two minutes and makes unsolicited telemarketing calls illegal under federal law.
- [ ] **[OptOutPrescreen](https://www.optoutprescreen.com/)**  
  The official joint opt-out site managed by Equifax, Experian, Innovis, and TransUnion. This cuts off unsolicited pre-approved credit card and insurance offers.  
  * **Electronic Opt-Out:** Lasts 5 years.  
  * **Permanent Opt-Out:** Submit online, then print and mail the signed confirmation form. Highly recommended to eliminate physical mail theft risk.
- [ ] **[DMAchoice](https://www.dmachoice.org/)**  
  Operated by the Association of National Advertisers (ANA). For a $5 fee (good for 10 years), you can stop direct mailings from companies you haven't explicitly done business with.  
  * Also submit your email to their [eMPS Email Opt-Out List](https://www.ims-dm.com/cgi/optoutemps.php).
- [ ] **[Valpak Direct Mail Opt-Out](https://www.valpak.com/remove-address)**  
  Stops delivery of the familiar blue envelope filled with local advertising coupons.
- [ ] **[Save.com (formerly RedPlum / RetailMeNot)](https://www.save.com/manage)**  
  Stops delivery of the thick weekly grocery and retail coupon bundle. You can also submit via their [OneTrust Opt-Out Form](https://privacyportal.onetrust.com/webform/45e4be25-919b-483f-9f95-12809576a2b3/6e633594-9a81-48bb-97ab-6fb29bf46019).
- [ ] **[Catalog Choice](https://www.catalogchoice.org/)**  
  A free nonprofit service that lets you search and unsubscribe from specific merchant catalogs and marketing mailers.
- [ ] **[USPS Informed Delivery](https://www.usps.com/manage/informed-delivery.htm)**  
  Receive a daily digital preview of incoming mail. This lets you monitor what arrives, spot lingering junk senders, and catch mail theft without sorting through paper.

> **Postal Mail Tip:**  
> First-Class and Priority Mail can be written **"REFUSED: RETURN TO SENDER"** and left unopened in your mailbox for the postal carrier to return for free.  
> However, bulk mail marked **"PRSRT STD"** (Presorted Standard) or **"Nonprofit Org"** does not include return postage; writing "Refused" on it does nothing. You must opt out directly using the links above.

## Phase 2: The Silver Bullet — Freeze Your Credit Reports
> **Estimated time:** 15 minutes  
> **Cost:** 100% Free by federal law  
> **Impact:** Maximum possible protection against financial identity theft.

Data brokers frequently expose full names, birth dates, past addresses, and relatives. If bad actors combine this with a leaked Social Security number from a corporate breach, they can open loans and credit cards in your name. 

Freezing your credit prevents anyone (including you) from opening new lines of credit until you lift the freeze. Freezing takes minutes, does **not** hurt your credit score, and does **not** affect existing accounts or cards.

Freeze all three major bureaus, plus the two critical secondary reporting agencies:

- [ ] **[Experian Security Freeze](https://www.experian.com/help/credit-freeze/)**
- [ ] **[Equifax Security Freeze](https://www.equifax.com/personal/credit-report-services/credit-freeze/)**
- [ ] **[TransUnion Credit Freeze](https://www.transunion.com/credit-freeze)**
- [ ] **[Innovis Security Freeze](https://www.innovis.com/personal/securityFreeze)** (The 4th national credit bureau, frequently targeted by fraudsters)
- [ ] **[ChexSystems Security Freeze](https://www.chexsystems.com/security-freeze/place-freeze)** (Used by banks to open checking and savings accounts; prevents fraudulent deposit accounts from being opened in your name)

## Phase 3: Prevention & Automated Removal Tools
> **Estimated time:** 10 minutes to set up  
> **Impact:** Saves 20–40 hours of manual data broker submissions and stops fresh leaks at the source.

Opting out manually works, but data brokers continuously re-scrape public records and recreate profiles. If you have the budget, automated removal and privacy masking services offer high return on investment.

### 🛡️ Recommended Tools

#### 1. [DeleteMe](https://joindeleteme.com/) — The Benchmark for Hands-Off Removal
* **What it does:** DeleteMe is the longest-running, dedicated personal data removal service. A team of privacy operators continuously submits opt-out requests and legal removals on your behalf to hundreds of data brokers and people-search databases.
* **Why it's recommended:** You receive an initial report within 7 days, followed by recurring privacy reports every few months documenting every site checked, records found, and removals completed.
* **Best for:** Anyone who wants a trusted, set-it-and-forget-it service to keep public people-search directories scrubbed clean.

#### 2. [Cloaked](https://www.cloaked.com/) — Prevention First (Masked Numbers & Emails) + Auto Removal
* **What it does:** Cloaked is an all-in-one privacy suite designed to solve the root problem: giving out your real information in the first place. It generates instant, disposable phone numbers (with call and text forwarding), email aliases, and virtual credentials for every shop, website, or form.
* **Why it's recommended:** It tackles both sides of the privacy coin. It includes built-in automated data broker removal to clean up historical records, while its masking technology ensures no new merchant can ever sell your real phone number or email address again.
* **Best for:** Anyone who wants proactive, daily privacy whenever they sign up for services, order online, or give out contact details.

#### 3. [Optery](https://www.optery.com/) — Best-in-Class Visibility & Free Scanning
* **What it does:** Optery scans hundreds of data broker platforms and provides visual screenshot proof showing exactly where your personal data, home address, and phone numbers are exposed online.
* **Why it's recommended:** Optery offers an exceptional **free scan tier**. Even if you don't purchase their automated cleanup plans, you can run their free scan to see precisely which brokers have files on you, complete with direct links to remove them yourself.
* **Best for:** Users who want total transparency, visual proof of removal, or a free roadmap for manual cleanup.

#### 4. [Incogni](https://incogni.com/) — Automated Legal Privacy Requests
* **What it does:** Developed by Surfshark, Incogni acts on your behalf using legal privacy frameworks (such as CCPA and GDPR) to send automated deletion demands to over 180 commercial data brokers, marketing lists, and financial recruitment databases.
* **Best for:** A budget-friendly, automated subscription that covers commercial marketing aggregators alongside people-search sites.

#### 5. Everyday Privacy & Masking Defenses
* **Email Aliases:** [SimpleLogin](https://simplelogin.io/), [Firefox Relay](https://relay.firefox.com/), or **Apple Hide My Email** (built into iCloud+). Never give your raw personal email address to retail stores, newsletters, or online accounts.
* **Virtual Payment Cards:** [Privacy.com](https://privacy.com/) lets you create merchant-locked virtual debit cards with custom spend limits, protecting your actual bank account and debit/credit card numbers from breaches.

> **⚠️ A Note on OneRep:**  
> OneRep was previously a popular opt-out tool. However, investigative reporting by Brian Krebs (KrebsOnSecurity) revealed that OneRep's founder was simultaneously running people-search site Nuwber. Due to this conflict of interest, tools like DeleteMe, Optery, or Cloaked are strongly recommended instead.

## Phase 4: High-Yield Manual Data Broker Opt-Outs
> **Estimated time:** 45–60 minutes  
> **Impact:** Removes your name, address, phone number, and relatives from the most visible search results on Google.

If you prefer the DIY approach instead of paying for a service, focus your time on the **parent aggregators** first. Many smaller people-search engines license their data from the same handful of mega-brokers.

### Copy-Paste Removal Request Template
When an opt-out page asks for an email explanation or provides an email contact, use this straightforward request:

```text
Subject: Data Removal and Opt-Out Request

My name is [Full Name], my mailing address is [Full Address], and my phone number is [Phone Number].

I am writing to formally request that you permanently opt me out and delete all of my personal information from your databases, search engines, and any third-party marketing, rental, or sales feeds.

This request covers my name, current and previous addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, and all associated metadata.

Please confirm receipt of this request and confirm once my personal records have been completely suppressed or removed from your systems.

Thank you.
```

### 1. The Super-Aggregators (Start Here!)
- [ ] **[PeopleConnect Suppression Center](https://suppression.peopleconnect.us/)**  
  **Highest single ROI for manual removal.** PeopleConnect is the parent company behind **TruthFinder, Intelius, Instant Checkmate, and US Search**. Submitting your suppression request here removes your public profile from all four major people-search websites at once.
- [ ] **[LexisNexis Consumer Portal](https://consumer.risk.lexisnexis.com/opt)**  
  LexisNexis is one of the world's largest commercial data brokers. Submit an opt-out request through their consumer portal to restrict disclosure of your public records.  
  * Also submit the [LexisNexis Direct Marketing Opt-Out](https://www.lexisnexis.com/privacy/directmarketingopt-out.aspx).
- [ ] **[Acxiom Privacy & Opt-Out](https://isapps.acxiom.com/optout/optout.aspx)**  
  Acxiom supplies commercial and marketing behavioral data to hundreds of corporate entities. Submit their opt-out form to suppress marketing profiles.

### 2. Major Standalone People-Search Engines
- [ ] **[Whitepages Suppression Request](https://www.whitepages.com/suppression-requests)**  
  Find your profile on Whitepages, copy the URL of your listing, paste it into the suppression tool, and verify via phone confirmation code.

  Search for your listing, copy your profile URL, and submit your email address for verification.

  One of the most frequently visited free people-search engines. Follow the removal instructions and verify your request via email.
