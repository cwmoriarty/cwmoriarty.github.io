## Fundamentals

1. **Never use your primary email or phone number to opt out.** Data brokers log verification inputs. Use a dedicated burner email or email alias, and a secondary VoIP number when phone verification is required.
2. **Never volunteer new information.** Only confirm details a broker already holds (or the minimum needed to match your record). Never upload a government ID unless legally required—and redact everything except your name and address if you do.
3. **Freeze your credit before chasing brokers.** Removing a people-search listing hides your public trail, but a credit freeze stops criminals from opening loans, bank accounts, or utility lines in your name.
4. **Understand how data flows.** Most small people-search sites buy from a handful of upstream aggregators, USPS change-of-address feeds, and retail loyalty programs. Stopping the upstream sources gives you the biggest return.
5. **Poison the pipeline going forward.** Deletion is only half the job. Using unique email aliases, masked phone numbers, virtual cards, and network tracker blocking prevents new profiles from forming.
6. **Use legal leverage.** Under federal laws (FCRA, TCPA) and state privacy laws (such as California's CCPA and Delete Act), opting out is a legal right with enforceable deadlines—not a favor you are asking.

## Phase 1: Eliminate Junk Mail & Phone Spam

- [ ] **[National Do Not Call Registry](https://www.donotcall.gov/)**  
  Register your mobile and landline numbers to make unsolicited telemarketing calls illegal under federal law.
- [ ] **[OptOutPrescreen](https://www.optoutprescreen.com/)**  
  Cuts off pre-approved credit card and insurance mailers from the major credit bureaus (5-year electronic opt-out, or permanent opt-out by mailing the signed confirmation form).
- [ ] **[DMAchoice](https://www.dmachoice.org/)** ($5 for 10 years)  
  Stops direct marketing mail from companies you have not bought from. Also submit your email to their [eMPS Email Opt-Out List](https://www.ims-dm.com/cgi/optoutemps.php).
- [ ] **[Valpak Direct Mail Opt-Out](https://www.valpak.com/remove-address)**  
  Stops delivery of the blue envelope of local advertising coupons.
- [ ] **[Save.com (formerly RedPlum / RetailMeNot)](https://www.save.com/manage)**  
  Stops delivery of the weekly grocery and retail coupon mailer (or submit via their direct [OneTrust Form](https://privacyportal.onetrust.com/webform/45e4be25-919b-483f-9f95-12809576a2b3/6e633594-9a81-48bb-97ab-6fb29bf46019)).
- [ ] **[Catalog Choice](https://www.catalogchoice.org/)**  
  Free service to search and unsubscribe from specific merchant catalogs and mailers.
- [ ] **[DirectMail.com Do Not Mail List](https://www.directmail.com/mail_preference/)**  
  Free opt-out list used by a broad network of commercial direct mailers.
- [ ] **[USPS Informed Delivery](https://www.usps.com/manage/informed-delivery.htm)**  
  Daily digital previews of incoming mail so you can spot lingering spam senders and catch mail theft without sorting paper.
- [ ] **Enable Phone Call Screening**  
  Turn on *Silence Unknown Callers* (iOS) or *Call Screen / Spam Protection* (Android), plus your mobile carrier's free spam-blocking filter (AT&T ActiveArmor, Verizon Call Filter, or T-Mobile Scam Shield).

> **Postal Mail & Moving Tips:**  
> Unopened First-Class and Priority Mail can be marked **"REFUSED: RETURN TO SENDER"** and left in your mailbox. Bulk mail marked **"PRSRT STD"** (Presorted Standard) or **"Nonprofit Org"** does not include return postage and cannot be refused—opt out directly using the links above.  
> **Moving Trap (NCOA):** When you file a USPS Change of Address, USPS licenses your new address through the National Change of Address (NCOA) database to commercial mailers and data brokers. When moving, update critical accounts (banks, employer, IRS) directly if you want to avoid triggering a fresh wave of broker listings.

## Phase 2: Freeze Your Credit & Consumer Reports

Freezing your credit and specialty consumer files blocks anyone from opening loans, credit cards, bank accounts, or utility/cell lines in your name—even if your Social Security number leaks in a breach. Freezing is **100% free** by federal law, does not affect your credit score, and does not affect existing accounts. You can temporarily thaw ("lift") a freeze in minutes whenever you apply for credit.

- [ ] **[Experian Security Freeze](https://www.experian.com/help/credit-freeze/)**
- [ ] **[Equifax Security Freeze](https://www.equifax.com/personal/credit-report-services/credit-freeze/)**
- [ ] **[TransUnion Credit Freeze](https://www.transunion.com/credit-freeze)**
- [ ] **[Innovis Security Freeze](https://www.innovis.com/personal/securityFreeze)** (4th national credit bureau)
- [ ] **[ChexSystems Security Freeze](https://www.chexsystems.com/security-freeze/place-freeze)** (Blocks fraudulent checking and savings accounts)
- [ ] **[NCTUE Security Freeze](https://nctueconsumerportal.com/)** (Blocks fraudulent telecom, mobile, pay-TV, and utility accounts)
- [ ] **[LexisNexis Security Freeze](https://consumer.risk.lexisnexis.com/freeze)** (Restricts specialty risk, insurance, and identity verification files)
- [ ] **[AnnualCreditReport.com](https://www.annualcreditreport.com/)** (Check free weekly reports from Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion to audit old addresses and spot unfamiliar accounts)

> **Protect Minors:** If you have children under 18, freeze their credit with Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion. Minors' SSNs are prime targets for synthetic identity fraud because nobody checks their credit for years.

## Phase 5: Outsmart Ad Tech, Search & Device Tracking

Removing your name from brokers does not stop apps, browsers, TVs, cars, and cell carriers from harvesting fresh behavioral profiles every day.

- [ ] **[Google "Results about you"](https://myactivity.google.com/results-about-you)**  
  Set up alerts and submit one-click removal requests whenever Google Search indexes your home address, phone number, or personal email. (Even before a broker deletes your listing, this hides it from casual searchers.)
- [ ] **[Global Privacy Control (GPC)](https://globalprivacycontrol.org/)**  
  Enable GPC in your browser (built into Firefox, Brave, and DuckDuckGo, or available via extension). It sends an automatic `Sec-GPC: 1` signal telling websites not to sell or share your data—legally enforceable in California and over a dozen other states.
- [ ] **[DAA WebChoices](https://optout.aboutads.info/)**  
  Scan your browser and opt out of behavioral tracking across participating ad networks (re-run if you clear cookies; note that the NAI retired its legacy opt-out tool in favor of GPC and OS controls).
- [ ] **Block Trackers at the Browser & DNS Level**  
  Use [uBlock Origin](https://ublockorigin.com/) (works best on Firefox) or Brave to strip ad scripts and fingerprinting pixels. For system-wide blocking across every app and device, configure encrypted DNS filtering with [NextDNS](https://nextdns.io/) or [AdGuard DNS](https://adguard-dns.io/).
- [ ] **Reset & Disable Mobile Ad IDs**  
  On **iOS**: *Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking* (turn off *Allow Apps to Request to Track*) and *Apple Advertising* (turn off *Personalized Ads*). On **Android**: *Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID*. Also revoke background location access for apps that do not strictly need it.
- [ ] **Opt Out of Mobile Carrier Tracking (CPNI)**  
  Wireless carriers monetize app usage, browsing metadata, and location trends unless you opt out: [AT&T Privacy Choices](https://www.att.com/PrivacyChoices) / [AT&T CPNI Opt-Out](https://www.att.com/cpni/optout), [Verizon My Privacy](https://www.vzw.com/myprivacy), and [T-Mobile Marketing & Privacy Choices](https://www.t-mobile.com/privacy-center/choices-and-controls/marketing-communications).
- [ ] **Disable Smart TV Surveillance (ACR)**  
  Smart TVs (LG, Samsung, Sony, Vizio, Roku) use *Automatic Content Recognition (ACR)* to fingerprint what you watch—even over HDMI—and sell viewing habits to ad networks. Disable *Viewing Information / ACR / Live Plus / Interactive TV* in your TV's privacy settings, or disconnect the TV from Wi-Fi and use a dedicated streaming box.
- [ ] **Turn Off Connected Car Telemetry**  
  Modern automakers collect driving telemetry (braking, speed, trip times) through connected apps and sell it to insurance risk brokers (LexisNexis and Verisk). Opt out of driving data sharing in your automaker's app and privacy portal, and request your consumer disclosure report from LexisNexis and Verisk to check what has already been collected.

## Phase 6: Preventive Hygiene (Stop Feeding the Pipeline)

Every time you hand over your real email, phone number, or debit card to a merchant, you restart the data broker cycle.

- [ ] **Use a Unique Email Alias for Every Account**  
  Use [SimpleLogin](https://simplelogin.io/), [Addy.io](https://addy.io/), [Firefox Relay](https://relay.firefox.com/), or Apple *Hide My Email*. When an alias starts getting spam, you know exactly which company sold or leaked your data and can turn that alias off instantly.
- [ ] **Never Give Your Real Phone Number to Businesses**  
  Keep your primary SIM number strictly for family, close contacts, and banking. Use a VoIP line (Google Voice, MySudo, or Cloaked) for store checkouts, deliveries, realtors, doctors' offices, and web forms. Switch sensitive accounts away from SMS 2FA to app-based TOTP or passkeys.
- [ ] **Use Virtual Payment Cards**  
  Use [Privacy.com](https://privacy.com/) (or your bank's virtual card feature) to generate merchant-locked cards with spend limits. This shields your real card number from merchant breaches and blocks cross-merchant purchase tracking.
- [ ] **Bypass the Retail Loyalty Card Trap**  
  Grocery and pharmacy loyalty programs (CVS, Walgreens, Kroger, Safeway) tie every in-person purchase directly to identity graphs like LiveRamp. Skip the loyalty card, use a burner/VoIP number, or use a store's generic courtesy number when discounts require a phone lookup.

## California Residents

California residents have strong statutory protections under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California DELETE Act (SB 362):

- [ ] **[California DROP (Delete Request and Opt-out Platform)](https://privacy.ca.gov/drop/)**  
  The official portal managed by the California Privacy Protection Agency (CPPA) to submit a single deletion request to all registered data brokers in California.
- [ ] **[California Data Broker Registry](https://cppa.ca.gov/data_broker_registry/)**  
  The CPPA's searchable registry of all registered data brokers, including direct links to their individual opt-out and deletion pages.
- [ ] **[Global Privacy Control (GPC)](https://globalprivacycontrol.org/)**  
  Legally binding browser signal in California that compels websites not to sell or share your personal data.

## Other State Privacy Rights

Over a dozen states now enforce comprehensive privacy laws giving residents the legal right to delete personal data and opt out of targeted advertising and data sales. States including **Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, Oregon, Montana, Delaware, New Jersey, New Hampshire, Nebraska, Maryland, and Minnesota** legally require businesses to honor automated **Global Privacy Control (GPC)** signals just like California. Residents of **Virginia, Utah, Iowa, and Indiana** also have statutory opt-out and deletion rights through company privacy portals. Look for the **"Your Privacy Choices"** link in website footers to exercise state-level deletion rights even if you do not live in California.
